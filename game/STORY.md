# Story bible: Nowhere Near Cambridge

**Setting:** Harvard, Massachusetts, October 1995. The real town's geography and landmarks, filtered through rumor. The tone is X-Files, Buffy or Stranger Things, with the supernatural kept understated.

**Premise:** Sam Dillard, 20s, comes home from Boston for Grandpa Ellis's funeral. A local guy, Rowan Fitch, has vanished on his bike. Since Grandpa died, the town has heard "the Hum" again.

**The secret:** In 1831, damming Bare Hill Pond flooded a road and the people living along it. The Dillards were the "Keepers." They kept the drowned "Kept" quiet with crested lanterns: a D wrapped around a flame. Grandma Ada still carries a lantern.

## Cast

- **Sam** (player): Glare, Grandpa's Advice, and Old Words (from the curios side quest).
- **Dusty**: the farm dog. Fetch, Growl.
- **Dani Reyes**: police officer. Freeze! Police!, Service Revolver.
- **Kit**: Rowan's kid brother. Slingshot, Pep Talk, Bottle Rocket.
- **Ada**: grandma.
- **Nell**: runs the general store.
- **The Chief**: police chief.
- **Mary**: librarian.
- **Abner**: cemetery hermit.
- **Lark**: Concordant at Fruitvale.
- **Deacon**: on the Common at night.
- **Patty's girl**: Kit's friend at the cemetery.

**Antagonists:**
- The Lantern Society (invented): hooded "Lantern Man."
- The Drowned Teamster, Amos Pell.
- The Hollow.
- Coveralls men near Fort Devlin.

## Chapter 1 beats

1. **Farm:** Ada gives Sam the flashlight; Dusty joins.
2. **Common:** the Chief, then Dani says to meet at Lovers Lane.
3. **Lovers Lane:** Rowan's tape in the culvert. Dani joins. The Hollow fight. Dusk falls.
4. **Library:** Mary, then the microfiche: "J. DILLARD … KEPT."
5. **Center Cemetery:** Kit joins. Knock on the crypt. Abner's story. Night falls.
6. **Bellview Burial Ground:** a ringing phone carries Rowan's voice from "under the road." The 1831 ledger page. Lantern Man boss. Sam gets the crested lantern.
7. **Bare Hill Pond dock:** Drowned Teamster boss. Ada appears with a lantern: "You should have stayed in Boston." End of Chapter 1.

## Side quests

- **Mary's curios:** blue bottle (cellar hole), hymnal page (Lark), ox shoe (beach sand). Reward: Old Words.
- **Lark's coffee:** reward is the hymnal page.
- **Patty's bottle rockets.**
- **Abner's cider:** reward is smelling salts.
- **Fort Devlin:** coveralls fight.

## Rules

- No real people's or groups' names. Never cast real religious groups as villains.
