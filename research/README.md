# Harvard, Massachusetts — Research Notes

This folder holds the background research behind *Harvard After Dark* (`../browser-after-dark/`) and the setting manuscript (`../Harvard, Massachusetts.md`). It is meant for game masters and writers running a Monster of the Week–style campaign in a fictionalized Harvard, MA.

## Contents

| File | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| [town-history.md](town-history.md) | A timeline from Nashaway land through the present day |

| [geography-and-map.md](geography-and-map.md) | Town layout, the OSM extraction method, the projection math, and the map's accuracy limits |
| [osm-landmarks.csv](osm-landmarks.csv) | 72 named OSM points (name, longitude, latitude) taken from the game's map data |

| [open-leads.md](open-leads.md) | Leads from the manuscript not yet researched or used, and future case ideas |

## Confidence labels used throughout

- **[Sourced]**: checked against a fetched source during research (listed in sources.md).
- **[OSM]**: taken from OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
- **[General]**: widely known background that was not re-checked against a source this session. Verify before presenting it as fact.
- **[Manuscript]**: a claim from the tongue-in-cheek setting manuscript. Treat it as folklore unless marked otherwise.
- **[Invented]**: made up for the game.

## Ground rules for using this material

- Do not use the real names of living people, families, religious communities, or companies in fiction. Use look-alike names instead (see fiction-key.md).
- Never attribute invented crimes, cannibalism, or occult practices to real people or communities. The spooky material belongs to fictional stand-ins.
- The manuscript's Indigenous "translations" and its "cursed land" explanations are not history. Do not repeat them as fact.
