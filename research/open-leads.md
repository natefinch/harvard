# Open Leads and Future Cases

## Manuscript headings with no content yet (not researched this session)
- **Hildreth House**: a historic house on the Common, used as a town senior center. [General; verify]
- **Bromfield House**: connected to the Bromfield family and the Bromfield School legacy. [General; verify]
- **Lion's Club** and the **Harvard Flea Market**: community events. Good for exposition and NPC introductions.
- **Bloody Hollow**: name origin unknown. Find where it is and why it is called that.
- **Appleworks building**: probably the old apple storage and processing building. Verify its location and what it is used for now.
- **Dillard Farm**: fictional stand-in for the Willard family farm.
- **Holy Hill**: probably the Shaker outdoor worship ground. OSM has a "Holy Hill" parcel near the Shaker Village at −71.5555, 42.5405.
- **Old Library**: the former library building, distinct from the Bromfield-school library now in use.
- **Lovers Lane** and **Cleaves Hill**: both real roads (OSM).
- **Cemetery next to high school**: Center Cemetery (see rumors).
- **Fiske Warren's Tahanto enclave**: find surviving buildings and boundaries.
- **The Feeneyite community**: verify current status before writing any stand-in.

## Case seeds
1. **The Single-Tax Ghost**: a Georgist colony that still collects "land value" from the dead.
2. **Woodcock Dusk**: the woodcock sky-dance at the Oxbow summons something that answers in a human voice.
3. **Project Beta Never Stopped**: the observatory's old SETI receiver logs a reply once a year.
4. **Sunday Shakers**: dancers seen through Shaker Village windows, in a village closed since 1918.
5. **Bed Rest**: the man buried in his bed on Poor Farm Road wants his bed back.
6. **The Servant's Stone**: a long-suppressed name behind the church, and a family that wants it to stay suppressed.
7. **Hell Pond Dive**: an underwater cave at Mirror Lake that connects to things it shouldn't.
8. **The Pencil Mill**: Benjamin Ball's Old Mill graphite carries a curse of erasure.
9. **Cyclists in Packs**: the hill roads take one rider every autumn.
10. **The 1918 Ward**: flu-era Devens barracks, and a doctor still doing rounds.
