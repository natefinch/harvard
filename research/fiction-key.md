## Case solution: *The Bell Beneath the Pond*
- Commonweal's resonator, carried through the groundwater, amplifies a historical "echo" of a road drowned in 1843. Rowan is trapped alive inside the reflected memory.
- Puzzle answers:
  - Crypt phone code: **184**
  - Console word: **ECHO**
  - Return melody: **LOW → HIGH → MIDDLE**
  - Carrier frequency: **184 Hz**
  - Mirror delay: **17 seconds**
- Resolution order: play the melody, call Rowan by name, then ring the brass bell. Two nonviolent endings are possible.
