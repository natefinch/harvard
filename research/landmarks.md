# Landmarks Gazetteer

Coordinates are from OpenStreetMap (lon, lat, WGS84), rounded. Full point list: [osm-landmarks.csv](osm-landmarks.csv). "Hooks" are story ideas, not facts.

## Harvard Center (the Common)
The Common is a classic New England town center: green, white church, store, library, and burial ground, all within a few hundred meters. The game uses a magnified inset because these points nearly overlap at town scale.

- **Harvard General Store**: −71.5839, 42.5005. The social hub, serving coffee, sandwiches, and gossip. Hook: the unofficial police dispatch, where every rumor is born or dies.
- **Harvard Center Cemetery**: −71.5842, 42.4998. The old burial ground by the Common and schools. The manuscript has kids peering into a crypt with flashlights to see "the moving corpse."
- **Harvard Public Library**: −71.5838, 42.4975. Housed in the converted **old Bromfield School**, a brick building behind a green field near the current schools, overlooking the east end of Bare Hill Pond. Manuscript rumor: the screams of a janitor locked in the basement one summer. The manuscript's library director has a secret occult collection.
- **Town Hall**: 13 Ayer Road. [Sourced]
- The **"Congo church"** (local slang for the Congregational church on the Common) has, per the manuscript, a lone headstone behind it for a servant buried there against the rules of the time.
- Main roads out: **Ayer Road** north to Devens and Route 2, **Still River Road** west, **Oak Hill Road** east, **Littleton County Road**, **Old Littleton Road**, and **Massachusetts Avenue**.

## Bare Hill Pond
- A reservoir (OSM relation 2722621, Wikidata Q49699912) south and southwest of the center, with many **islands**. **Dam**: −71.5955, 42.4972. **Public access / town beach area**: −71.5999, 42.4797. **Bare Hill Wildlife Sanctuary**: −71.597, 42.4717.
- The pond was raised by damming. The manuscript says wagons are still on the bottom from the flooding, and that carts went through the ice.
- Hooks (used in the game): a drowned 1843 road and the lost Bellweather bell.

## Still River (village)
A hamlet in the west along **Still River Road**, below the ridge, facing the Nashua floodplain. It has an old depot (Still River Depot Road) and the Harvard Depot Road area.
- **Bellevue Cemetery**: −71.6048, 42.4950. Manuscript: a crypt where the last occupant had a **telephone line installed** so she could call from the other side. Also a **phantom buggy** on Still River Road on Halloween.
- **St. Benedict Abbey at Still River**: −71.6211, 42.4869. Also nearby: the **Sisters of St. Benedict Center** (−71.6216, 42.4860) and **St. Benedict Chapel** (−71.6222, 42.4828). Benedictines with Latin chant, on a hill above the river. The manuscript lists the "Slaves of the Immaculate Heart of Mary" separately; see the history file. *Fictionalize fully.*
- **Holy Hill**: the manuscript lists it among Still River topics, but OSM shows "Shaker Village Gp–Holy Hill" near the Shaker village (−71.5555, 42.5405). Historically, Shaker communities kept outdoor worship sites called Holy Hills. Research further before relying on this.

## Fruitlands (Prospect Hill)
- **Fruitlands Museum**: −71.6111, 42.5047. **Farmhouse**: −71.6114, 42.5098. **Shaker Museum** (the relocated 1794 office): −71.6109, 42.5087. Address: 102 Prospect Hill Road.
- The hillside looks west over the Nashua valley toward Wachusett and Monadnock. The property is 210 acres with trails down into the Oxbow lands.
- Hooks: a modern-day transcendentalist commune that never left (with absolutely no coffee), and manuscript-adjacent "Fruitlands cannibals" folklore, which is a joke about the diet. Keep it with fictional stand-ins.

## Shaker Village (northeast)
- **Historic District**: −71.5543, 42.5359, along **Shaker Road** and **South Shaker Road**. These are private homes today.
- **Shaker Cemetery**: −71.5554, 42.5314. The Shakers used uniform, simple markers.
- **Stone Barn**: −71.5586, 42.5299. **Quarry**: −71.5574, 42.5268.
- **Carpenter's Shop**: −71.5537, 42.5349. **Shaker Reservoir** (presumed site): −71.5533, 42.5382.
- **Millpond**: −71.5542, 42.5473. **Spring House / Springhouse**: −71.5445, 42.5243. **Spring Slate Marker**: −71.5484, 42.5304.
- **Shaker Hills Country Club**: −71.5519, 42.5424.
- Note that a separate **Shirley Shaker Village** sits just over the line in Shirley/Devens, at about −71.6495, 42.5293, with its own cemetery.
- Hooks: celibate dancers ("Shaking Quakers"), gift drawings, spirit-messages, and songs said to be received from angels. Used in the game as the "farewell song."

## Oak Ridge Observatory (Pinnacle Road)
- About −71.5576, 42.5054, at 42 Pinnacle Rd. The Harvard (university) George R. Agassiz Station, active 1933–2005.
- Its **61-inch Wyeth reflector** was once the largest telescope east of Texas. The observatory discovered 38 minor planets and ran **optical SETI** and **Project BETA** (a radio SETI search). Late work focused on exoplanets.
- The manuscript calls it the "Abandoned Harvard Observatory." Hooks: a SETI receiver that heard something, and domes that still turn at night.

## Orchard country (east)
- **Carlson Orchards**: −71.5553, 42.5086 (several polygons nearby). It lies off Oak Hill Road and Pinnacle Road and is famous for pick-your-own and cider. The manuscript calls it "Carlton Orchards" and invents Chester and Brandi Carlton. The game renames it **Carrow Orchard**.
- Other orchards line Oak Hill, Prospect Hill, and Still River roads. The **"Appleworks building"** in the manuscript is a reference to the town's apple processing past; see open-leads.md.

## Poor Farm Road
- Around −71.567, 42.5228. The name comes from the town poor farm, the 19th-century almshouse. Manuscript: a headstone for a man who died in an epidemic, **buried in his bed** because no one would touch the body.

## Devens / Shabikin (northwest)
- **Mirror Lake**: −71.6129, 42.5260. **Little Mirror Lake**: −71.6082, 42.5243. **Mirror Lake Road**: −71.6137, 42.5277.
  - Mirror Lake is a kettle pond with a trail network. The manuscript calls it "Hell Pond" and says it is 150 ft across and 80+ ft deep, with an underwater cave. That is folklore; the real pond is a deep glacial kettle, and the depth figures are unverified.
- **Commonwealth Fusion Systems (SPARC)**: −71.6260, 42.5422. Fictionalized as **Commonweal Research**.
- **Oxbow Schoolhouse**: −71.6087, 42.5408. **Fort Devens Post Cemetery**: −71.6219, 42.5288.
- Devens streets are named for battles (Antietam, Balls Bluff, Malvern Hill, El Caney, Cavite, Bulge, Givry, Saratoga) and generals (Patton, Sheridan, MacArthur). These are great flavor names for a "base that never closed" plot.

## Oxbow National Wildlife Refuge (west)
- **Harvard parking**: −71.6254, 42.4969. The refuge covers the floodplain and meanders of the Nashua River, which forms the town's western boundary. It was established in 1974.
- Wildlife includes the **American woodcock** (sky-dance displays at dusk) and the **Blanding's turtle** (rare, long-lived). Habitats are floodplain forest, wet meadows, and oxbow ponds.
- Hooks: a river hermit, herons, and things moving in the oxbows at dusk.

## Other named features worth knowing
- **Roads**: Cleaves Hill Road and **Lovers Lane** (both named in the manuscript as dangerous for cyclists, and both real in OSM), East and West Bare Hill Road, Old Shirley Road, Prospect Hill Road, Pinnacle Road, Poor Farm Road, Harvard Depot Road, Still River Depot Road, Old Mill Road, Woodchuck Hill Road, Snake Hill Road, Kettlehole Road, Codman Hill Road, Under Pin Hill Road, Stonecutters Path, and Nonesuch Way.
- **Water**: Nashua River, Still River, Nonacolcus Brook, Black Spruce Bog, Robbins Pond, Grove Pond, Plow Shop Pond, Sandy Pond, Balch Pond, Eldridge Pond, Frog Pond, and many NHESP vernal pools. Some of these lie in neighboring towns inside the map's bounding box.
